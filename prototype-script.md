# Prototype Script

## Project Name

Library Quest

## Purpose of This Script

This script provides a sample walkthrough for the Library Quest voice user interface. It can be used for a live demo, usability testing, or presentation. The goal is to show how the assistant guides a user through genre, mood, content avoidance, length, and recommendation refinement.

## Scenario

The user wants a fantasy book that feels dark and atmospheric, but they do not want anything too depressing or emotionally heavy.

## Demo Conversation

### Opening

Assistant: Welcome to Library Quest. What kind of reading quest are you starting today?

User: I want a fantasy book.

Assistant: Great. What kind of fantasy quest are you looking for: cozy, adventurous, dark, romantic, mysterious, or something else?

User: Something dark, but not too depressing.

Assistant: Got it. You want a darker fantasy, but not something emotionally overwhelming. Are there any themes you want to avoid, such as graphic violence, major character death, horror, sexual content, or heavy grief?

User: Avoid major character death and heavy grief.

Assistant: Understood. I will avoid major character death and heavy grief. Do you want a short, medium, or long read?

User: Medium.

Assistant: Based on your quest, I recommend The Lantern Road. It is a medium-length fantasy with a mysterious and adventurous tone. It includes magical danger and family secrets, but avoids graphic violence, major character death, and heavy grief.

User: Can you give me another option?

Assistant: Sure. Another option is Tea with the Moon Witch. It is shorter and cozier, with light romance, magical cottage settings, and gentle conflict. Would you like something darker than that, or does cozy fantasy sound better?

User: I want something a little darker.

Assistant: Then The Lantern Road is the better match. It keeps the mysterious fantasy atmosphere without becoming too emotionally heavy.

User: That sounds good.

Assistant: Quest complete. Your recommended book is The Lantern Road.

## Alternate Path: User Gives a Vague Answer

Assistant: Welcome to Library Quest. What kind of reading quest are you starting today?

User: I do not know.

Assistant: That is okay. Do you want something comforting, exciting, romantic, mysterious, strange, or emotionally intense?

User: Something comforting.

Assistant: Great. Would you like fantasy, romance, contemporary fiction, mystery, or something else?

User: Fantasy.

Assistant: Do you want a short, medium, or long read?

User: Short.

Assistant: Based on your quest, I recommend Tea with the Moon Witch. It is a short cozy fantasy with a comforting mood, light romance, and gentle conflict.

## Alternate Path: User Gives Unsupported Input

Assistant: What genre are you interested in: fantasy, science fiction, romance, mystery, horror, literary fiction, or something else?

User: I want cookbook fantasy.

Assistant: I do not have cookbook fantasy as a category yet, but I can help with fantasy, cozy books, romance, mystery, horror, science fiction, or literary fiction. Would you like me to search for cozy fantasy instead?

User: Yes, cozy fantasy.

Assistant: Great. Do you want the story to feel romantic, adventurous, funny, or calm?

## Alternate Path: User Changes Their Mind

Assistant: What kind of mood are you looking for: cozy, adventurous, dark, romantic, emotional, funny, or strange?

User: Dark.

Assistant: Got it. Are there any themes you want to avoid?

User: Actually, I do not want dark. I want something cozy.

Assistant: No problem. I will switch the mood from dark to cozy. Do you want a cozy fantasy, cozy mystery, or cozy romance?

## Alternate Path: User Wants to Stop

Assistant: Do you want a short, medium, or long read?

User: Stop.

Assistant: Ending your quest. You can come back anytime when you are ready to search for your next read.

## Notes for Demo

This prototype script is designed to show:

* How the assistant starts the interaction
* How the user chooses genre and mood
* How the assistant asks about content avoidances
* How the assistant handles vague answers
* How the assistant handles unsupported input
* How the assistant lets the user refine a recommendation
* How the assistant ends the interaction politely

## What This Demonstrates

Library Quest is not only a book search tool. It is a guided recommendation experience. The voice interface helps users describe the kind of reading experience they want, even when they do not know a specific title, author, or keyword.
